- **Faster estimating** with AI-powered takeoff tools

## Best Construction Software by Category

### Best Overall: Procore

Procore remains the gold standard for construction project management. It's the one platform that scales from mid-size contractors to enterprise GCs.

**Strengths:**

- End-to-end project controls (RFIs, submittals, daily logs, financials)
- Strong field app for supers and PMs
- Massive integration ecosystem
- Robust executive dashboards

**Watch out for:**

- Higher price point than SMB solutions
- Requires formal onboarding for full value
- Can overwhelm occasional users

### Best for Small Contractors: JobTread

JobTread delivers enterprise-grade features at a small-business price. Unlimited users on every plan makes it a no-brainer for growing contractors.

**Strengths:**

- Unlimited users = unlimited adoption
- Modern, intuitive interface
- Strong across estimating, project management, and financials
- Outstanding customer support

**Watch out for:**

Togal.AI is changing the game for estimators. Its AI-powered takeoff detects and measures construction elements from PDF plans in minutes—work that used to take hours.

**Strengths:**

- Computer vision improves with use
- Works across multiple trades

Bluebeam Revu is the construction industry's gold standard for PDF markup and document control.

## How to Choose the Right Software

Not every tool is right for every contractor. Here's how to pick:

### 1. Start With Your Biggest Pain Point

Don't try to solve everything at once. What's costing you the most time or money right now?

- **Estimating too slow?** → Togal.AI, STACK, Esticom
- **Field-office communication breaking?** → ServiceTitan, JobTread, Procore
- **Losing track of job costs?** → JobTread, Knowify, Procore
- **Document chaos?** → Bluebeam Revu

### 2. Match Size to Scope

- **1-10 employees:** JobTread, Jobber, FieldEdge
- **10-50 employees:** JobTread, ServiceTitan, Knowify
- **50+ employees:** Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud
